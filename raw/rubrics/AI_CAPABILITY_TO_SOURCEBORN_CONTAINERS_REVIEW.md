### Summary Statistics (Review View)

- Families with strong primary mapping to reasoning / knowledge containers (CON-041–CON-048, CON-034, CON-078): high density  
- Families with strong primary mapping to executive / control / metacognition containers (CON-028–CON-032, CON-075, CON-077): high density  
- Families with strong primary mapping to tool / external / social containers (CON-023, CON-069–CON-072, CON-080): high density  
- Pure biological containers (CON-001–CON-008, CON-011, CON-012, CON-015, CON-021, etc.) receive only weak or indirect links — this is expected and preserved  
- No capability family was forced into a container solely to achieve coverage
