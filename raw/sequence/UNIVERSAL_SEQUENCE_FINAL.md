# UNIVERSAL SEQUENCE — FINAL GRAMMAR

This is the complete, self-contained definition of the Universal Sequence.  
It incorporates every correction, every named mechanism, every loop routing, every barrier, every threshold type, and every Mahabharata-derived primitive that has been locked.  

It also states the remaining structural flaws so that they remain visible and cannot silently re-enter the model.

---

## Operating Definition

A Sequence is the complete uniquely identified history of how a declared prior reality became a declared new reality.  

It includes everything that was required, formed, triggered, activated, connected, blocked, supplied, transformed, executed, verified, remembered and closed for that result.  

The visible event is almost never the beginning of the Sequence.  

Many Sequences begin, form results, store latent consequences, or create seeds long before the carrier that will later execute them exists, before any actor becomes aware of them, and before the moment at which a local timeline expects them to start.  

This is ordinary structure, not an exception.

---

## Core Invariants (Never Violated)

**Barrier Law**  
No dependent edge may cross while a required Sub-Sequence return remains unaccepted. Independent branches may continue. The parent may remain open or suspended. The barrier is local to the dependent edge.

**No In-Place Loop**  
A Sequence never re-enters a stage it has already left by simply restarting the same Sequence. Every repetition opens a new Sequence with a new unique identity.

**No Reopen**  
A closed Sequence stays closed. New evidence creates a new Sequence that references the closed one. History is never rewritten.

**Closure is a Sequence Word**  
Sequence Closure Status and Entity Outcome are two separate records. An entity may persist, degrade, transform or terminate while the Sequence that examined it closes. These two statuses are never collapsed into one.

**Encounter is Conditional**  
Two Sequences meeting is never assumed. Encounter is an explicit, conditional stage that may or may not be required.

**Never Manufacture a Source**  
If a required input has no recoverable origin, the gap remains a gap and is handed to the human. The model never invents a source to make the graph look complete.

## Pass Zero — Declare the End First

Before any reverse or forward walk begins, the end that is being explained must be named.

The end is the only thing that decides which earlier reality matters and which is noise.  

Without a declared end the reverse walk expands forever.

The end also sets the resolution level. A Sequence about one meal does not automatically require reconstruction of the entire universe. If a required dependency cannot be explained without moving further backward, the horizon expands. Depth is determined by necessity.

## The Main Line Stages

Every stage below is a named object that can be referenced, thresholded, ledgered and closed.  

Full detail is given under each name.

### Prior Reality

Everything that was already true before this particular Sequence began.  

It contains physical conditions, existing entities, previous results, stored memories, old rules, skills, relationships, resources, environments, previously closed Sequences, and Sequence Seeds that have not yet bound a carrier.  

Prior Reality is the sum of older closed Sequences plus current conditions. Nothing is required to originate inside the present Sequence.

### Origin / Source

Where the required starting conditions came from.  

Possible classes: physical, biological, informational, social, technical, institutional, mathematical, relational, inherited, previous Sequence result, or unknown.  

If something is used but has no origin, it is an unconnected dot. The reverse pass must either find the source or explicitly retain the gap. It must never invent a source.

Material, Energy and Information are recorded here as distinct provenance layers when they are relevant.

### Environment / Host

The substrate, conditions and constraints in which the Sequence runs.  

An identical entity in a different environment produces a different Sequence. Environment is therefore causal structure, not decoration.  

Some environments are themselves the closed results of earlier Sequences.

### Formation Sequence

How the followed object became available at all.  

It may be assembled, grown, emerged, inherited, transformed, designed, generated, represented, already present, or unknown.  

A Sequence Seed can exist before any Sequence Carrier. If no intentional actor yet exists, the formal object is Sequence Seed, Future Condition, Latent Dependency or Stored Commitment. It is incorrect to call every early condition “intent.”

### Existence

The snapshot of what exists now: boundary, identity and state.  

Boundary records where the thing stops and what is absent.  
Identity records the continuity criteria that decide how much change is still the same thing.  
State records the readable condition at any point.

### Stability / Viability

Whether the thing can keep going.  

Yes means continuation.  
No means collapse, repair or terminate.  

This is the Entity Outcome, not the Sequence closing. Collapsing these two statuses was the worst error in earlier versions.

### Requirement Emerges

Why anything would change.  

The driver origins are: Natural / Constraint Dynamics, Need, Want, External Demand, Goal, Opportunity, Curiosity / Unknown, Damage / Deviation, Relational Driver, Represented Future State, Stored Commitment / Promise, Previous Result.  

Want sits beside Need; it is never folded into Need. Gravity has Natural Dynamics and no driver at all. That is a real value, not a missing one.

### Requirement

What is actually needed, stated in the open.  

This is the concrete demand that later stages must satisfy or fail against.

### Dependency Graph

What must already be true for the next transition.  

Dependencies may be resource, condition, capability, information, other entity, infrastructure, prior result, prior closed Sequence, memory, time, location, permission or knowledge.  

Each is marked required, optional or alternative.

### Availability

Whether the required dependency is accessible.  

If not, three Sub-Sequences may open side-by-side: Locate, Build, Substitute.  
A fourth Sub-Sequence, Re-Angle, may open when a new angle set is required.  

The main line is held at a barrier until a required return is accepted. The node closes when any sibling is accepted. They are three (or four) distinct Sub-Sequences, not one node run three times.

### Qualification

Whether the candidate fits.  

If it does not, a new Sub-Sequence may open to repair, condition, replace or investigate.

### Testing

Whether the candidate holds under examination.  

Each test is itself a closed Sub-Sequence. Failure does not restart the same attempt; it opens a new repair or investigation Sub-Sequence.

### Selection

Which one among the qualified candidates.  

Priority and arbitration decide when multiple rules or pressures apply.

### Decision / Choice

Who decided and by what priority.  

The Controller is recorded here; it is never assumed.

### Acquisition / Encounter

Whether the required relation is met.  

Encounter is conditional. Two Sequences meeting is never an assumption. An explicit threshold must fire.

### Boundary Approach

Arrival at the edge of the followed object or system.

### Intake / Coupling

The states now affect each other.  

Threshold Range records that the boundary has been reached.

### Transformation / Conditioning

Something is made fit before main processing.  

Any transformation may itself require a Sub-Sequence.

### Processing

The actual work and how far the work carries.  

Propagation is recorded when relevant.

### Assimilation / Incorporation

Something becomes part of the new state, or does not.

### Integration

The same event as the row above, tagged, not deleted.  

Integration records that the change has been absorbed into the larger structure.

### Action

What the Sequence does outward.

### Output

What leaves it.

### Return

What goes back to the wider system.

### Result

What changed.  

A Result may be latent: it exists and has not yet fired. Waiting is itself a state.

### Impact

What changed for everyone else.  

Tiny local events can carry critical global-closure weight later. This is Downstream-Critical Change.

### Verification

Checked against the Requirement, not against the wish.  

A Repair Sub-Sequence may open here. It closes when the repair is done and is accepted only when the Requirement is met.  
Threshold Confidence records that verification has returned.

### Feedback

What reality taught.  

This can end the whole class of Sequence or open a new one that inherits the lesson.

### Memory

What is kept.  

Failure Memory records what must not be done again.

### Memory Validation

Whether the memory is true and by how many independent witnesses.  

A Second Witness Sub-Sequence may open.  
Threshold Count-Quorum records that a second independent account has been obtained.  
Averaging two witnesses that differ is forbidden; the gap remains a gap and is handed to the human.

### Compression

What survives being shortened.  

Every compressed handle must carry scope, conditions, confidence, recoverability, source and known exceptions. Compression without these fields corrupts meaning.

### Inheritance

What is handed on.  

Biological, individual / social / technical, and physical channels are recorded separately when relevant.  
Rule Inertia is the case where a rule keeps running after the reason for it is gone.

### Sequence Closure

The Sequence itself reaches a terminal status.  

Possible values: Success, Failure, Partial, Unknown, Unavailable, Not Applicable.  

The Sequence can close only when the Open-Sequence Ledger is empty of required open returns for this Sequence.

### Entity Outcome

What happened to the followed entity.  

Possible values include: persists, coheres, degrades, transforms, splits, merges, is consumed, terminates, a new entity is instantiated, is absent, is unknown, or is not applicable.  

None of these values is the word “closed.” Entity Outcome and Sequence Closure remain separate.

### Next-Sequence Seed

A new Sequence may be generated from the closed result.  

The new Sequence references the closed one. The closed one stays closed.

### Re-Entry Point

Where a later Sequence starts, not where the previous Sequence stopped.  

Threshold Event records that the seed has been accepted.

### Execution

The new Sequence runs on its own ledger row.

### Re-Verify

Against the corrected Requirement.

### Memory Update

The ledger row closes and the declared end is checked.  

Did the walk arrive at the reality that was named at the start?

## The Nine Named Loops

Every loop lands as a new Sub-Sequence.  
None returns inside the stage it left.

**Availability Loop**  
Fires when Locate fails, then Build fails. Lands back at Availability with a new Sub-Sequence.

**Qualification to Selection Loop**  
Fires when the first test fails. Lands at Selection with a new candidate Sub-Sequence.

**Testing to Decision Loop**  
Fires when the structure proves too steep or the candidate does not hold. Lands at Decision / Choice.

**Collapse / Repair Loop**  
Fires when the entity degrades. Lands at Requirement Emerges with driver origin Damage. The Entity Outcome opens a fresh Sequence.

**Verification to Requirement Loop**  
Fires when result does not equal Requirement. The Requirement is restated; it is never quietly patched.

**Feedback to Prior Reality Loop**  
Fires when every pyramid was robbed or the whole class of Sequence ends. The next one begins somewhere else; the gap is not hidden.

**Memory Validation to Information Loop**  
Fires when one witness only is present. Two witnesses that differ produce a Halt; the gap is the Mask and is handed to the human.

**Compression to Memory Loop**  
Fires when the method is not recoverable. A defect in a record remains a defect; the whole point in a body is preserved only if the handle carries its exceptions.

**Memory Update to Origin / Source Loop**  
Fires when the fact is named. The new Sequence references the closed one; there is no reopen.

Close condition is never the same as acceptance condition.

## Open-Sequence Ledger

Every currently active Sequence is registered with its identity, parent, node, required flag, controller, contract and status.  

The ledger is the only mechanism that turns the Barrier Law into an executable condition.  

A Sequence may close only when every required return for that Sequence has been accepted.

## Threshold Types on Edges

Thresholds live on edges, never inside stages as a substitute for the edge law.

- Time — the window opens; this is why the transition fires now  
- Event — first load applied, candidate present, seed accepted  
- Absence — maintenance stops  
- Range — boundary reached  
- Confidence — verification returns  
- Count-Quorum — second independent witness obtained  
- Value, State, Composite (and, or, unless) — used when the condition requires them  

A trigger is the observation that evaluation is possible.  
A threshold is the condition that must be true before the edge is allowed to fire.  
They are not the same thing.

## Known Structural Flaws That Remain Visible

These flaws are not hidden. They are recorded so they cannot silently return.

1. The main line is still long. Fifty-seven named stages create cognitive load. Some stages may later prove to be pure annotations rather than executable nodes. The grammar does not yet contain a formal reduction rule that preserves all invariants while shortening the line.

2. Threshold typing is edge-specific but not yet machine-checked against a closed vocabulary. New threshold kinds can still be invented without formal registration.

3. The nine loops are named and routed, yet the conditions that fire each loop are still expressed in natural language. They are not yet reduced to pure threshold expressions that a ledger can evaluate without human reading.

4. Knowledge Distribution (Actor View) is acknowledged as a primitive but has no first-class ledger structure. The model still assumes a single shared reality unless an explicit Actor View annotation is added by hand.

5. Sequence Seed before Carrier is allowed, yet the ledger has no native row type for a seed that has no bound entity. Such seeds must currently be stored as ordinary open Sequences with a null carrier, which is an awkward encoding.

6. Multi-scale Closure Scope is declared, but the inheritance rules between nested scopes (action inside promise inside character inside era) are not yet formalised beyond documentation.

7. Compression handles require six mandatory fields (scope, conditions, confidence, recoverability, source, exceptions). The grammar states the requirement but does not yet enforce it at the moment a handle is created.

8. The model still relies on human declaration of the end. There is no internal mechanism that can propose candidate ends from an undifferentiated stream of events.

These eight points are the remaining open surface.  
They are not denied.  
They are the next work.

## Final Statement

The Universal Sequence is the complete uniquely identified history of how a declared prior reality became a declared new reality.  

Every transition requires an explicit threshold.  
Every blocked dependency opens a contracted Sub-Sequence rather than an in-place loop.  
Every Sub-Sequence and Main Sequence remains ledger-traceable.  
Every Sequence terminates with a Closure Packet that records both Sequence status and Entity Outcome.  
Knowledge may be distributed across actors.  
Seeds may exist before any carrier is born.  
Small events may carry critical global weight.  
Lower rules may be broken only under recorded higher contracts.  
The closed result becomes trace, capability, environment, inheritance, law or seed for later Sequences.  

Nothing required may remain unlinked.  
Nothing closed may be reopened.  
Nothing invented may be offered as a source.

This is the grammar.
